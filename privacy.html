Linework

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Linework is a free flowchart editor that runs in your browser at exinics.github.io/linework. It is a personal project, made by the GitHub user exinics. It has no ads and no tracking, and it doesn’t sell or share your data. This page explains what it stores and why.

Using Linework without signing in

You can use Linework without an account. Your current diagram and settings (theme, presets, panel layout and export options) are kept in your browser’s local storage on your own device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It turns on My charts, which saves your diagrams online, and syncs your style presets between your devices. When you sign in with Google, Linework receives from Google:

  • your name and email address, shown in the top bar so you know which account you’re using;
  • your profile picture link and a unique account ID, which Linework uses to keep your charts separate from everyone else’s.

Linework doesn’t ask for any other access to your Google account. It can’t see your email, files or contacts.

What is stored online

If you are signed in, Linework stores the following in Google Firebase (Firestore), under your account ID:

  • the charts you save to My charts: their names, the diagram, a small preview image, and when they were created and last changed;
  • your shape and arrow style presets.

Access rules let only you read or change this data. Firebase Authentication also keeps a record of your sign-in (your Google account ID, name, email and sign-in times).

Services Linework uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may log your IP address when you load the page. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google Firebase handles sign-in and stores the data above. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts, so your browser asks Google for them when the page loads. See Google’s privacy policy.

Linework uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your charts and presets stay until you delete them. You can delete any chart yourself in My charts, and download a backup of all of them there first if you like. To have your whole account removed, including your presets and sign-in record, open an issue at github.com/exinics/linework/issues. All your data will be deleted. You can also remove Linework’s access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/exinics/linework/issues.
